## There is no support desk

No phone number, no support address, no chat window, no hours. This site has
none of those, and it is better to say so on a page you can find than to leave
you looking for a contact link that is not there.

That is a consequence of what this is rather than an oversight — see
[How this site works](/help/how-this-site-works).

## Who can help with a booking

Nobody here, and nobody at an airline either, because a booking made here is
not known to any airline. There is nothing for anyone to look up.

If you are here because of a real trip booked somewhere else, the airline that
issued that ticket is who to ask, and every airline we list has a page with its
own details — see [Contacting an airline](/help/contacting-an-airline).

## Telling us an article is wrong

Every help article ends with a thumbs up and a thumbs down, and that is the one
channel that works. It is anonymous, it takes one click, and the counts order
the help links in the footer — so a page that keeps getting a thumbs down sinks
down the list rather than sitting at the top being unhelpful.

It cannot carry a message, which is its limitation. What it can do is say
which page did not answer the question.

## The fare-alert list is not a contact form

The form at the foot of every page keeps an email address and sends nothing to
it. It is not a way to reach anybody and nobody reads it — see
[The fare-alert list](/help/fare-alerts).
