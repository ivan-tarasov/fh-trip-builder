## Keeping one

Every result carries a save control. Use it and the flight joins the list
under [Saved](/my/saved), which is the shortlist to compare against rather
than a booking of any kind — nothing is held or reserved by saving it.

## The list is priced fresh

What gets kept is which flights you picked, and nothing about them. Prices and
times are read again every time you open the page, so a saved flight always
shows what it costs now rather than what it cost when you saved it.

That is the useful part. A shortlist that quietly showed last week's fares
would be worse than no shortlist, because you would only find out at checkout.

## It belongs to this browser

The list lives in this browser, like your currency choice. There are no
accounts here, so it does not follow you to a phone, and clearing your browsing
data clears it. Left alone, it keeps for a year — which is longer than your
bookings last, since those go with the browsing session and not with the
browser. See [Ticket did not arrive](/help/ticket-not-received).

## When one disappears

Flights come and go, and this site regenerates its network. If a flight you
saved is no longer sold it simply drops off the list rather than sitting there
unbookable. Nothing else on the list is affected, and the fix is to search the
route again — see [Searching for flights](/help/searching-for-flights).
