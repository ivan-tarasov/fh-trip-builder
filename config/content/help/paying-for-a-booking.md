## Nothing is charged

This site is a demonstration. The checkout asks for a card because a booking
flow without one would not be much of a demonstration, but no payment is taken,
no bank is contacted and no money moves. Whatever you enter, your account is
untouched.

## No card is kept

The number you type is used to work out two things — which scheme the card
belongs to, and its last four digits — and those are all that is stored with
the booking. The number itself is never written down anywhere, so the receipt
shows you "ending 1234" and there is nothing else to show.

A card is accepted if it passes the checksum every real card number satisfies.
That is a shape test, not an approval: it means a mistyped digit is caught
before you reach the end of the form, not that anybody agreed to pay anything.

## The price is fixed before the card

The fare, the taxes and the total appear before the payment step and do not
change during it. If you travel with children the total already reflects what
each traveller pays, so the figure by the Pay button is the figure for the
whole party. See [Prices and currencies](/help/prices-and-currencies) for how
that total is converted.

## What you get afterwards

A booking reference on the confirmation page, and the booking under
[My bookings](/my/bookings) in the browser you used. Nothing is emailed — see
[Ticket did not arrive](/help/ticket-not-received), which is the article about
what that does and does not mean.
