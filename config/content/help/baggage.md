## Every fare includes a personal item

One bag that fits under the seat in front of you, on every fare we sell.
Nothing else about baggage is fixed. A carry-on bag and a checked bag both
depend on the fare you pick.

## Check the fare, not just the price

Each result lists what its fare allows under **What’s included**, before you
fill anything in. Six lines: personal item, carry-on bag, checked bag, changes,
cancellation and seat choice. Each one is marked included, for a fee, or not
included.

The same six appear again at checkout, and once more on the booking afterwards,
so you can always check what you bought.

## On a connection, the tightest flight decides

A journey allows only what its strictest leg allows. If the second flight
includes no checked bag, the journey includes no checked bag — even where the
first flight would have carried one.

That is why the list at checkout is one list per direction rather than one per
flight.

## Weights, sizes and extra bags

Those belong to the airline, and they change by route and by cabin. We do not
hold them, so check before you pack. Every airline we sell has a page here with
its phone number on it — see [all airlines](/airlines).

Extra bags are bought from the airline, not from us.
