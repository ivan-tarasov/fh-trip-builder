## Where the control is

Open [My bookings](/my/bookings). Each booking that has not flown yet carries a
cancel button, and pressing it asks you to confirm before anything happens. A
booking that has already flown, or that you cancelled earlier, does not offer
one.

## What cancelling does

It marks the booking cancelled. That is the whole of it: the booking stays on
the list with a cancelled stamp on it, moves to the cancelled tab, and stops
counting down to departure.

No money moves, because none moved when you booked — see
[Paying for a booking](/help/paying-for-a-booking). Nothing is sent to the
airline either. This site takes bookings and keeps them; it does not hold a
seat anywhere, so there is nothing to give back and nobody to tell.

## It has to be the same session

A booking belongs to the browsing session it was made in, and so does the
right to cancel it. Close the browser and that session ends: the booking still
exists, but the list is empty and the cancel button is not there to press.

There are no accounts here, so there is no way to prove a booking is yours
once the session has gone. If you need to be able to cancel, do it before you
close the browser.

## Cancelling twice

The second attempt says the booking was not found or is already cancelled.
Both halves of that are worth reading: it is the same answer you would get for
a booking that was never yours, because from the outside those two look
identical.
