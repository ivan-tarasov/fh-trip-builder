## Where it is

Open [My bookings](/my/bookings) and then the booking itself. Its own page
carries a panel headed Receipt, alongside the flights and the contact details
given at checkout.

## What it shows

The fare, the taxes and fees, and the total, each in the currency the booking
was made in — with the currency's own code printed beside the total so there is
no guessing which dollar it means. Below that, the card: its scheme and its
last four digits, which is all of a card this site ever keeps.

A cancelled booking still shows its receipt. The total is labelled differently
there, because what it records is what the booking was for rather than
something still owing.

## What it is not

It is not an invoice and not a tax document. There is no VAT breakdown, no
supplier details and no invoice number, because there was no sale — nothing was
charged, so there is nothing to account for. See
[Paying for a booking](/help/paying-for-a-booking).

If you came here looking for something to give to an accounts department, this
is not it, and no version of this site can produce one.

## It reads in its original currency

The figures do not follow the currency switcher. A booking keeps the currency
it was made in and the rate that applied at the time, so a receipt read months
later says what it said on the day — see
[Prices and currencies](/help/prices-and-currencies).

## For as long as the session lasts

The receipt is part of the booking, and the booking is only listed while the
browsing session that made it is alive. Close the browser and there is no page
to open — see [Ticket did not arrive](/help/ticket-not-received).
